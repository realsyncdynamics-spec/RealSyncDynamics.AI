# Governance Runtime Layer — Strategic Positioning

**Core Thesis:** Others help deploy AI faster. RealSync helps operate AI safely.

RealSync Dynamics.AI is the **Governance Runtime Layer** for Enterprise AI — not a deployment tool, but a continuous operational control plane for AI systems in regulated industries.

## The Fundamental Shift

| **Traditional AI Governance** | **RealSync Governance Runtime** |
|---|---|
| Compliance checklist at deployment | Continuous governance as infrastructure |
| Audit trail after incidents | Immutable evidence collected in real-time |
| Reactive risk detection | Proactive risk monitoring across all AI actors |
| Policy as document | Policy as enforced code |
| Manual evidence assembly | Automated provenance & lineage tracking |
| Per-model compliance | Enterprise-wide AI system governance |

## Six Governance Pillars

1. **Continuous Risk Monitoring** — Real-time detection of drift, bias, model degradation, policy violations across all AI deployments
2. **Immutable Audit Trail** — Every decision, intervention, and change recorded with context, exportable for regulators
3. **EU AI Act Enforcement** — Automated classification, risk-tier routing, documentation, and compliance gates
4. **DSGVO Data Governance** — Consent tracking, purpose limitation, data lineage, erasure orchestration
5. **Policy-as-Code** — Governance rules executable, not advisory; guardrails enforced at runtime
6. **Provenance & Lineage** — Complete visibility into AI system inputs, training data, modifications, and outputs

## Target Markets (Six Regulated Industries)

### Finance (Banks, Fintechs, Asset Managers)
- **Pain:** Credit decisioning, fraud detection, trading algorithms all under regulatory scrutiny
- **RealSync Value:** Real-time fairness monitoring, audit trail for regulator review, policy enforcement on model updates

### Healthcare (Hospitals, Insurance, Diagnostics)
- **Pain:** Diagnostic AI, treatment recommendation engines require GDPR + specialized healthcare data governance
- **RealSync Value:** Patient consent tracking, data lineage, immutable evidence for audits

### Insurance
- **Pain:** Underwriting algorithms, claims processing under discrimination/fairness requirements
- **RealSync Value:** Continuous bias monitoring, policy enforcement, audit evidence for regulators

### Public Sector & Government
- **Pain:** Public services using AI must be transparent, auditable, non-discriminatory by law
- **RealSync Value:** Governance infrastructure for citizen-facing AI systems

### HR & Talent Management
- **Pain:** Recruitment, performance algorithms under GDPR and anti-discrimination law
- **RealSync Value:** Consent management, fair-hiring enforcement, audit trail

### Industry 4.0 (Manufacturing, Logistics)
- **Pain:** Autonomous systems, predictive maintenance AI under safety & data regulations
- **RealSync Value:** Continuous monitoring, safety guardrails, compliance evidence
